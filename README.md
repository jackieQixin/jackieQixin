# Personal Research Journey
My journey with robotics began at Chongqing University. It was here, within the Key Laboratory of Mechanical Engineering, that I first encountered robotics — a concrete-and-steel romance of engineering. Starting from scratch, We built robots ranging from rehabilitation robots to quadruped robots. This experience served as my foundational enlightenment, igniting my passion for robotics. It was also where I first witnessed the remarkable power of reinforcement learning for quadruped robot locomotion.

I am deeply grateful to Prof. Chao Huang at the University of Southampton. Under his guidance, I deeply dove into reinforcement learning research. I learned to cultivated deeper curiosity and critical thinking toward scientific questions. I experienced the beauty of pure scientific inquiry and the immense charm of reinforcement learning, laying a solid academic foundation for my subsequent doctoral studies. During this process, I collaborated with Prof. Donglin Wang and MiLab at Westlake University on humanoid, VLA, world models with reinforcement learning. I was fortunate to meet many outstanding collaborators here, whose insights greatly benefited my research.

I later joined Prof. Lin Shao in National University of Singapore, my current PhD supervisor, to explore embodied AI, WAM (World Action Model), and real-world RL for loco-manipulation.

My long-term vision is to develop lifelong-learning embodied agents adaptable to real-world settings. I hope robots can enter ordinary homes to deliver elderly care and uphold human dignity, especially by supporting marginalized groups.

We are looking for collaborators. If you take ownership and aspire to produce meaningful work, please get in touch. Relevant prior experience is not mandatory — we can grow together. I am always thankful to the collaborators, mentors and seniors who have guided and accompanied me along this journey.
